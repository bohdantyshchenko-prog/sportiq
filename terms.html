Closed Beta Terms

Effective date: August 13, 2026.

Purpose

NOVIQ is provided for closed product testing and sports-learning purposes. The current build may use curated demonstration data and may change during the beta.

Accounts and local preview

The beta may allow either a cloud account or Local Preview. Local Preview is tied to browser storage on the current device. Cloud Account data can synchronize to NOVIQ infrastructure when cloud configuration is enabled.

No outcome guarantee

NOVIQ is designed to improve decision review, not to guarantee sporting outcomes. It must not be treated as betting, financial, medical, legal, or other professional advice.

Beta reliability

The beta may contain defects or incomplete integrations. Users should export any local progress they consider important. Service availability, external providers, and cloud synchronization are not guaranteed during closed testing.

Feedback

Feedback may be used to improve NOVIQ. In the current build, beta feedback and diagnostic telemetry remain on the device unless the user deliberately exports or shares a beta report.

Data control

Users can reset local data and, when signed in, request deletion of NOVIQ product data stored for their cloud profile. Deleting NOVIQ product data does not currently delete the Supabase authentication identity.

These closed-beta terms require professional legal review before any public paid release.
